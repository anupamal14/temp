5×5 Column Swap Word Game

Puzzle Player mode

Tap two cells in the same column (not the first column) to swap their rows in that column. First column is fixed. Goal is to form all 5 target words horizontally.

Create or share a puzzle Designer / host mode

1) Enter 5 target words (or pick a random set). 2) Click "Apply words" to see the solution. 3) Click "New random shuffle" to scramble columns 2–5. 4) Click "Generate share link" and send the URL to friends. When they open it, they get the scrambled puzzle with your words.
